About this view

The playhead follows the audio clock. Events use arranged musical time, not original protocol latency. Resets can have interpolated positions. CTS recordings show measurement cases rather than inferred protocol packets. Accompaniment can continue between events. The animated bars illustrate playback and power; they are not a measured audio spectrum.

Open files are processed locally. No upload, analytics, or external assets. Video export is not included in this version.
